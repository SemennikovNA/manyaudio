Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

ManyAudio is a macOS app that lets you change the volume of individual apps, switch audio output and input devices, and connect paired Bluetooth devices. We do not collect, store or share your personal data.

Data the app collects

None. ManyAudio has no accounts, analytics, ads or third-party SDKs. The app does not connect to the internet.

What is stored on your Mac

To remember your preferences, ManyAudio stores the following locally on your Mac: per-app volume and mute, per-device output volume, interface language, the list of music players and other settings. This data is never sent anywhere and is removed together with the app.

System Audio Recording permission

macOS requires this permission for an app to change the volume of another app. ManyAudio only uses the audio while it plays, to make it quieter or louder. Audio is never recorded, saved or transmitted.

Bluetooth permission

Bluetooth access is used only to show your paired headphones and speakers and to connect the one you choose. This information is not stored or transmitted.

Children

The app does not collect data from any users, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date.

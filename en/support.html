Support

If something doesn't work or you have an idea, email semennikovna@yandex.ru. Please include your macOS version and what exactly happens.

An app's volume doesn't change

Make sure ManyAudio is allowed to record system audio: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen & System Audio Recording → System Audio Recording Only. Relaunch ManyAudio after granting the permission.

An app isn't in the list

The list shows apps that are playing audio. Start playback and the app will appear.

Headphones don't connect from Available

Make sure the headphones are on and nearby, and for AirPods the case is open. Check that ManyAudio has Bluetooth access in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Bluetooth.
